# ChildColor

**Culture visuelle et création artistique des enfants**

Website: https://childcolor.eu/

ChildColor is an international cultural programme dedicated to the ways
children perceive, interpret, and represent the world around them through
drawings, paintings, and other artistic creations.

## About the Project

ChildColor brings together artistic creations by children from different
countries and gives them a place within contemporary visual culture.

The programme explores the relationships between childhood, visual
expression, cultural environments, imagination, and shared human
experiences.

## Main Themes

- Children's visual expression
- Drawing, painting, and artistic creation
- Cultural diversity and international dialogue
- Childhood experiences and personal perspectives
- Family, friendship, nature, dreams, and everyday life
- Digital collections and cultural preservation
- Educational and artistic initiatives
- Exhibitions and cultural partnerships

## Activities

ChildColor develops and supports:

- Digital collections
- Presentations and exhibitions
- Artistic activities
- Educational projects
- Cultural partnerships
